# Welcome to SupplyGuard AI! 👋

Your AI-powered supply-chain disruption and recovery assistant.

SupplyGuard AI helps procurement managers, operations analysts and incident coordinators turn a supplier delay into a defensible recovery plan, while people keep control of every purchasing decision.

### How can I help you?

- 📦 **Operational impact**: usable stock, affected products, production commitments and customer orders at risk.
- 📄 **Evidence**: contracts, qualification records, procedures, supplier notices, e-mails and past incidents, with sources.
- 🔗 **Dependencies**: suppliers, upstream manufacturers, components, factories and customers.
- ⚖️ **Recovery options**: inventory transfers, expedited delivery, alternative suppliers, reallocation and rescheduling, compared on cost and risk.
- ✅ **Approval**: the exact action, quantities, costs and evidence for your decision. Nothing is ordered without approval.

### Get Started

Describe the supplier disruption in the chat below.

**Example:** *"Supplier Atlas Components has announced a 12-day delay for component MC-17. Determine which customer commitments are at risk, investigate alternative sources, and recommend a recovery plan. Additional spending must not exceed €75,000. Do not create a purchase order without approval."*

Include the supplier, the component and the length of the delay; if anything is unclear, I will ask before investigating.
